# Magic
First and foremost in Mundus magic is not widespread. Most all people treat magic with distrust and disdain. Many kingdom's will treat magic with outright hostility. There are some exceptions.
Celestial Magic - Accepted almost everywhere
Wilderness Magic - Accepted among Elves, Halflings, and Gnomes
Arcane Magic - Accepted among Gnomes
Abyssal Magic - Not Accepted anywhere
# Species
### [[Humans]]
Created by [[Adam]] god of Peace, War, Diplomacy, and Conquest.
Humans are the most common species.
People will be predisposed to judge you purely on vibes.
### [[Dwarves]] 
Created by [[Odin]] god of Riches, Death, Wanderers, and Judgement.
Dwarves are the second most common species.
You will be seen as fairly trustworthy with a penchant for collecting riches and being fine drinking companions.
### [[Elves]]
Created by [[Hera]] goddess of Agriculture, Fertility, Secrets, and the Moon.
Elves are the third most common species.
People will tend to be wary of you and treat you as a mystical creature.
### [[Halflings]]
Created by [[Frodo]] god of Home, Food, Messengers, and Thieves.
Halflings are the fourth most common species.
People regard you as a homebody and someone who rarely ventures far from home.
### [[Gnomes]]
Created by [[Pike]] goddess of Loyalty, Trickery, Love, and Lust.
Gnomes are the fifth most common species.
People will see you as someone who lives wild lives full of adventure, magic, and much companionship.
### [[Orcs]]
Created by [[Azog]] god of Wilds, Survival, Nature, and Loss
Orcs are the least common species.
People will attempt to treat you with much respect and attempt to keep their distance.
### Lineage
Lineages are extra features that go on top of base species.
[[Dragonborn]]
- Insanely rare and treated with awe and fear. Generally seen as an omen of disaster to come.
Physical Attributes - Scales, Claws, Wings, Dragon Snout -
 - Scales - While Unarmored AC = 13 + Dex mod
 - Claws - Unarmed strikes become 1d4 + Str mod slashing
 - Wings - Gain a fly speed that equals base speed
 - Dragon Snout - Gain Breath weapon
[[Aasimar]]
- Insanely rare and treated with awe and respect. Generally seen as a good omen.
Physical Attributes - Wings, Celestial Resistance, Healing Hands, Inner Radiance, Necrotic Shroud -
 - Wings - Gain a fly speed that equals base speed
 - Celestial Resistance - Resistance to Necrotic damage and Radiant damage
 - Healing Hands - As a Magic action, you touch a creature and roll a number of d4s equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The creature regains a number of Hit Points equal to the total rolled. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
 - Inner Radiance - Searing light temporarily radiates from your eyes and mouth. For the duration, you shed Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet, and at the end of each of your turns, each creature within 10 feet of you takes Radiant damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
 - Necrotic Shroud - Your eyes briefly become pools of darkness, and flightless wings sprout from your back temporarily. Creatures other than your allies within 10 feet of you must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC 8 plus your Charisma modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or have the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.
[[Tiefling]]
- Insanely rare and treated with fear and distrust.
Physical Attributes - Horns, Red Skin, Tail, Fiendish Eyes -
 - Horns - Resistance to Poison Damage and gain Poison Spray cantrip, Level 3 gain Ray of Sickness, Level 5 Hold Person.
 - Red Skin - Resistance to Necrotic Damage and gain Chill Touch cantrip, Level 3 gain False Life, Level 5 Ray of Enfeeblement.
 - Tail - Resistance to Fire Damage and gain Fire Bolt cantrip, Level 3 gain Hellish Rebuke, Level 5 Darkness.
 - Fiendish Eyes - Gain the Thaumaturgy cantrip.
[[Changeling]]
- Unheard of, no records of someone seeing a changelings true form.
Physical Attributes - Form Shifter, Changeling Instincts, Fey Charisma -
 - Form Shifter - Gain the ability to change your appearance. You are unable to access other non-changeling features while shape changed.
 - Changeling Instincts - You gain proficiency with two of the following skills of your choice: Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion.
 - Fey Charisma - Gain +2 to Charisma score and a -2 to Constitution score.
[[Goliath]]
- Extremely rare, usually outcasts within their settlements.
Physical Attributes - Giant Size, Cloud's Jaunt, Fire's Burn, Frost's Chill, Hill's Tumble, Stone's Endurance, Storm's Thunder -
 - Giant Size - Your size is large, you have Advantage on Strength Checks, your speed increases by 10 feet. You cannot wear normal armor and have a -2 to Dex score.
 - Cloud's Jaunt - As a [Bonus Action](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#bonus%20action_xphb), you magically teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.
 - Fire's Burn - When you hit a target with an attack roll and deal damage to it, you can also deal 1d10 Fire damage to that target.
 - Frost's Chill - When you hit a target with an attack roll and deal damage to it, you can also deal 1d6 Cold damage to that target and reduce its [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
 - Hill's Tumble - When you hit a Large or smaller creature with an attack roll and deal damage to it, you can give that target the [Prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_xphb) condition.
 - Stone's Endurance - When you take damage, you can take a [Reaction](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#reaction_xphb) to roll 1d12. Add your Constitution modifier to the number rolled and reduce the damage by that total.
 - Storm's Thunder - When you take damage from a creature within 60 feet of you, you can take a [Reaction](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#reaction_xphb) to deal 1d8 Thunder damage to that creature.
[[Genasi]]
- Extremely rare, usually outcasts within their settlements.
Physical Attributes - Unending Breath, Lightning Resistance, Mingle with the Wind, Earth Walk, Merge with Stone, Fire Resistance, Reach to the Blaze, Acid Resistance, Amphibious, Call to the Wave -
 - Unending Breath - You can hold your breath indefinitely while you're not [incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_phb).
 - Lightning Resistance - You have resistance to lightning damage.
 - Mingle with the Wind - You know the [shocking grasp](https://5e.tools/spells.html#shocking%20grasp_phb) cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the [feather fall](https://5e.tools/spells.html#feather%20fall_phb) spell with this trait, without requiring a material component. Starting 5th level, you can also cast the [levitate](https://5e.tools/spells.html#levitate_phb) spell with this trait, without requiring a material component. Once you cast [feather fall](https://5e.tools/spells.html#feather%20fall_phb) or [levitate](https://5e.tools/spells.html#levitate_phb) with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast either of those spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this race).
 - Earth Walk - You can move across [difficult terrain](https://5e.tools/quickreference.html#bookref-quick,3,difficult%20terrain) without expending extra movement if you are using your walking speed on the ground or a floor.
 - Merge with Stone - You know the [blade ward](https://5e.tools/spells.html#blade%20ward_phb) cantrip. You can cast it as normal, and you can also cast it as a bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Starting at 5th level, you can cast the [pass without trace](https://5e.tools/spells.html#pass%20without%20trace_phb) spell with this trait, without requiring a material component. Once you cast that spell with this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of 2nd level or higher.
 - Fire Resistance - You have resistance to fire damage.
 - Reach to the Blaze - You know the [produce flame](https://5e.tools/spells.html#produce%20flame_phb) cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the [burning hands](https://5e.tools/spells.html#burning%20hands_phb) spell with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can also cast the [flame blade](https://5e.tools/spells.html#flame%20blade_phb) spell with this trait, without a material component. Once you cast [burning hands](https://5e.tools/spells.html#burning%20hands_phb) or [flame blade](https://5e.tools/spells.html#flame%20blade_phb) with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast either of those spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
 - Acid Resistance - You have resistance to acid damage.
 - Amphibious - You breathe air and water.
 - Call to the Wave - You know the [acid splash](https://5e.tools/spells.html#acid%20splash_phb) cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the [create or destroy water](https://5e.tools/spells.html#create%20or%20destroy%20water_phb) spell with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can also cast the [water walk](https://5e.tools/spells.html#water%20walk_phb) spell with this trait, without requiring a material component. Once you cast [create or destroy water](https://5e.tools/spells.html#create%20or%20destroy%20water_phb) or [water walk](https://5e.tools/spells.html#water%20walk_phb) with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast either of those spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
[[Beastborn]]
- Extremely rare, seen as products of tampering with Wilderness magic.
Physical Attributes - Wings, Talons, Long-Limbed
 - Wings - Gain a fly speed equal to walking speed can't use this flying speed if you're wearing medium or heavy armor.
 - Talons - Unarmed strikes become 1d6 + Strength mod slashing damage.
 - Long-Limbed - When you make a melee attack on your turn, your reach for it is 5 feet greater than normal.
 - Hooves - You have hooves that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. If you move at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hit it with a melee weapon attack on the same turn, you can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one attack against the target with your hooves.
 - Lucky Footwork - When you fail a Dexterity saving throw, you can use your reaction to roll a d4 and add it to the save, potentially turning the failure into a success. You can't use this reaction if you're [prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_phb) or your speed is 0.
 - Rabbit Hop - As a bonus action, you can jump a number of feet equal to five times your proficiency bonus, without provoking opportunity attacks. You can use this trait only if your speed is greater than 0. You can use it a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
 - Mimicry - You can accurately mimic sounds you have heard, including voices. A creature that hears the sounds you make can tell they are imitations only with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check against a DC of 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.
 - Bite - You have a fanged maw that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. You can throw yourself into a feeding frenzy. As a bonus action, you can make a special attack with your Bite. If the attack hits, it deals its normal damage, and you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
 - Lizard Scales - You have tough, scaly skin. When you aren't wearing armor, your base AC is 13 + Dexterity modifier. You can use your natural armor to determine your AC if the armor you wear would leave you with a lower AC. A shield's benefits apply as normal while you use your natural armor.
 - Horns - You have horns that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. Immediately after you take the [Dash](https://5e.tools/actions.html#dash_phb) action on your turn and move at least 20 feet, you can make one melee attack with your Horns as a bonus action.
 - Mirthful Leaps - Whenever you make a long jump or a high jump, you can roll a d8 and add the number rolled to the number of feet you cover, even when making a standing jump. This extra distance costs movement as usual.
 - Cat's Claws - You can use your claws to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
 - Cat's Talent - You have proficiency in the Perception and Stealth skills. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness only as shades of gray.
 - Feline Agility - Your reflexes and agility allow you to move with a burst of speed. When you move on your turn in combat, you can double your speed until the end of the turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you move 0 feet on one of your turns.
 - Shell Defense - Your shell provides you a base AC of 17 (your Dexterity modifier doesn't affect this number). You can't wear light, medium, or heavy armor, but if you are using a shield, you can apply the shield's bonus as normal. You can withdraw into your shell as an action. Until you emerge, you gain a +4 bonus to your AC, and you have advantage on Strength and Constitution saving throws. While in your shell, you are [prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_phb), your speed is 0 and can't increase, you have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws, you can't take reactions, and the only action you can take is a bonus action to emerge from your shell.

# Class
The class you choose will affect how people approach interactions with you.
Barbarian - Weary and with Care
Bard -Arcane- Open arms and accepted in most circles
Cleric -Celestial- If apart of that persons practice very welcoming, if not then welcome but weary and avoidant.
Druid -Wilderness- Avoided and viewed as a wild person.
Fighter - Treated as a warrior and respected or feared by most.
Monk - Treated as a mystery and attempt to avoid.
Paladin -Celestial- If apart of that persons practice very welcoming, if not then welcome but weary and avoidant.
Ranger -Wilderness- Avoided and viewed as a wild person.
Rogue - General viewed as a normal person unless obviously criminal
Sorcerer -Arcane or Abyssal- Viewed normally unless obviously magical
Wizard -Arcane- Viewed normally unless obviously magical
Warlock -Celestial or Abyssal- Viewed normally unless obviously magical
